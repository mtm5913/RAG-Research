# Partner review

**Status: real partner feedback pending.** AI assistance is not a substitute for partner review. No issue, change or retest has been invented.

## Give your partner this route

[Overview](./overview.md) → [sources](./sources.md) → [approach comparison](./research.md) → [source trace and evaluation plan](./experiments.md).

## Ask about the decisions

- Can you trace one permitted source through representation, retrieval and a proposed answer?
- Do the questions justify the selected approach compared with the baseline?
- Which evaluation question would expose missing or incorrect evidence?
- What assumption or source detail needs clarification before implementation?
